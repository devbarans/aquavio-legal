# Aquavio Support

**English** · [Türkçe]({{ "/tr/" | relative_url }}) · [Deutsch]({{ "/de/" | relative_url }}) · [Русский]({{ "/ru/" | relative_url }}) · [العربية]({{ "/ar/" | relative_url }})

Aquavio brings the daily route, client records, water testing, checklists, photos and PDF visit reports together in one app for pool service professionals.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Where is my data stored?**  
All your client, visit and photo records are stored only on this device; no account is needed. The app works without an internet connection. Map tiles and finding a location from an address need a connection.

**How are visits scheduled automatically?**  
When you choose visit days in the client form, visits for the next 7 days are added to your route automatically.

**How does route optimisation work?**  
The optimise button on the Route screen orders stops to shorten the total driving distance. If you allowed location access, the route starts where you are. Add locations to your clients to use optimisation.

**How is chemical stock deducted?**  
Amounts of stock products you add during a visit are deducted from stock automatically when the visit is completed. If you edit a completed visit, stock is updated by the difference.

**How do I send the report to my client?**  
Send the PDF report created when a visit is completed from any app with 'Share PDF', or send your client a summary message with 'WhatsApp'.

**What happens to an unfinished visit?**  
What you enter is saved instantly. Use 'Continue' on the visit card on the Home screen to pick up where you left off.
