# Aquavio – Privacy Policy

**English** · [Türkçe]({{ "/privacy/tr/" | relative_url }}) · [Deutsch]({{ "/privacy/de/" | relative_url }}) · [Русский]({{ "/privacy/ru/" | relative_url }}) · [العربية]({{ "/privacy/ar/" | relative_url }})

**Effective date:** 18 November 2026

This policy explains how the Aquavio iOS app handles your information. Aquavio is developed by Baran Baran. The same policy is available in the app under Menu > Help and Support > Privacy Policy.

## Summary

- Everything you enter is stored only on this device.
- Aquavio has no server; the developer cannot access your data.
- There is no account, analytics, advertising or tracking.
- No third-party software libraries (SDKs) are used.

## Information Stored

- Profile and business details: name, company name, phone, email, address and tax details. Used in the header of PDF visit reports and in the QR code.
- Client details: client and contact names, contact details, address, location (coordinates), pool and equipment details, prices and notes.
- Visit records: water readings (including pH, free and combined chlorine, salt and filter pressure), chemicals used, checklist, skip reasons, notes, issue reports and photos.
- Business records and preferences: service packages, chemical stock, checklist template, appearance and reminder settings.
- Records are kept in the app's local database and photos in the app's Application Support folder. iCloud sync is not used.

## Photos

- The camera is used only to take photos during a visit; photos taken are not saved to your photo library.
- Adding photos from your library uses the iOS system photo picker; Aquavio only accesses the photos you select.
- Photos are saved as a resized copy; metadata from the original file, such as location, is not carried over.

## Location

- Location is used only when you start an action: to set the starting point when optimising a route, to show your position on the map and to save that point when you tap "Use My Current Location" in the client form.
- Your location is not tracked in the background and no location history is kept.
- When you look up a location from an address, the address text is sent to Apple's location service. Maps and directions are provided by Apple Maps.

## Notifications

- If you turn on reminders, a daily visit summary is shown with a local notification scheduled on your device. The notification includes the number of visits and up to three client names. Push notifications are not used.

## Sharing

- Aquavio never shares information on its own. Information leaves the device only through actions you start.
- PDF visit reports and CSV files are created on your device in the app's temporary folder and shared through the share sheet with the app or person you choose.
- The WhatsApp and call options open the corresponding app; a message is sent only when you send it.
- If the database can't be opened, "Back Up Data" lets you export a copy of your records only to an app or location you choose.

## Information Not Collected

- As the developer, we do not collect any information from you or your device. The app has no user accounts, analytics tools, advertising, tracking or crash reporting services.

## Retention and Deletion

- Your information stays on your device until you delete it.
- Deleting a client also deletes that client's visits, photos, equipment and issue reports.
- Menu > Settings > Delete All Data permanently deletes all client, visit, photo, issue, equipment, service package and chemical stock records. Profile and business details are kept.
- Deleting the app removes all information Aquavio has stored. If iCloud or computer backups are turned on, iOS may include app data in your device backups.

## Information About Your Clients

- The client information you store is entirely under your control. You are responsible for handling it in line with applicable data protection law, such as the GDPR, the UAE Personal Data Protection Law or Turkey's KVKK.
